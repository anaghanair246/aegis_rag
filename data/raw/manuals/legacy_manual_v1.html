Aegis Series-7 Hydraulic Control System — Operator Manual

Document AEG-OM-700, Revision 1 (superseded). Applies to software revisions 3.0 and 3.1 only.

1. Overview

The Aegis Series-7 Hydraulic Control System provides closed-loop hydraulic pressure regulation for industrial press lines. The system is built around the Hydraulic Power Pack, which supplies pressurized fluid to the press circuit under the supervision of the Aegis Controller.

2. Key Components

ReferenceComponentFunction
Hydraulic Power PackHydraulic Power Pack (HPU)Generates and regulates hydraulic pressure
IV-21Isolation Valve 21Isolates the HPU from the press circuit
PS-04Pressure Sensor 04Reports HPU discharge pressure to the controller

4. Starting the Hydraulic Power Pack

Before starting, verify:

  • Hydraulic fluid level is within the normal band.
  • Isolation valve IV-21 is open.
  • Emergency stop is reset.
WARNING: Do not start the unit with the maintenance access panel removed.
  1. Turn the POWER selector to ON.
  2. Press START.
  3. Verify pressure reaches 180 bar.

5. Normal Operating Ranges

Normal HPU discharge pressure is 180 bar, as measured by pressure sensor PS-04.

6. Alarms

See the Aegis Alarm Reference for alarm A17 (Hydraulic Pressure Low) and related codes.
